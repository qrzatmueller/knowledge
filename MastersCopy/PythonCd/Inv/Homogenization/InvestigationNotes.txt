 Conclusion on RKU. It is not a homogenization approach, but just a fast way
 to approximate the loss factor of the composite material.
 Since it is only taking into account the bending stresses, it misses out on
 all the shear losses. which can be very much relevant. therefore underestimating
 the loss factor. a better homogenization technique or another way to validate
 my model is in order.
